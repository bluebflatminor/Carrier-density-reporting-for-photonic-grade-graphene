Carrier-density reporting for photonic-grade graphene

A work-in-progress standards proposal, Oct 12, 2026.

Work in progress. This is a developing proposal with a supporting evidence annex, compiled with AI assistance and pending a human archive pass. Values, statuses and references may change.

The proposal

Extend IEC TS 62607-6-10, or add a companion part, so that graphene films intended for photonic use report:
  1. Carrier density and mobility separately, not only sheet resistance.
  2. For electrical methods, a measured gate capacitance and the test structure used, rather than an assumed dielectric constant.
  3. Interband absorption at the operating wavelength, with the assumed Fermi velocity stated.

Why it matters

At 1550 nm, graphene's interband absorption is Pauli-blocked once its Fermi level exceeds about 0.40 eV, roughly 1.2×10¹³ carriers per cm². Published wafer-scale processes leave residual doping anywhere from near zero to about 2×10¹³ cm⁻², and at least two sit at or across that edge before any gate is applied. Whether a film absorbs at the operating wavelength therefore depends on ordinary process choices: transfer method, resist, passivation.

Existing standards cover structure (ISO/TS 21356 series) and sheet resistance (IEC TS 62607-6-10). None specifies carrier density, Fermi level, or absorption at the operating wavelength.
