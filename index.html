Carrier-density reporting for photonic-grade graphene

A work-in-progress standards proposal, Oct 12, 2026. Revised Nov 3, 2026.

Work in progress. This is a developing proposal with a supporting evidence annex, compiled with AI assistance and pending a human archive pass. Values, statuses and references may change.

The proposal

Add a photonic-grade profile, as a companion part or an extension of the IEC TS 62607-6 series (6-10, 6-16, 6-23 and 6-32), so that graphene films intended for photonic use report:
  1. Carrier density and mobility separately, not only sheet resistance, for pristine graphene on wafer-scale films and device test structures.
  2. For electrical methods, a measured gate capacitance and the test structure used, rather than an assumed dielectric constant.
  3. Interband absorption at the operating wavelength, with the assumed Fermi velocity stated.

Why it matters

At 1550 nm, graphene's interband absorption is Pauli-blocked once its Fermi level exceeds about 0.40 eV, roughly 1.2×10¹³ carriers per cm². Published wafer-scale processes leave residual doping anywhere from near zero to about 2×10¹³ cm⁻². One unencapsulated process crosses that edge before any gate is applied, and another sits within about 0.03 to 0.11 eV of it. Whether a film absorbs at the operating wavelength therefore depends on ordinary process choices: transfer method, resist, passivation.

Gated modulators can absorb part of this as a Dirac-voltage offset, at a cost in drive voltage. In one chip-scale study the residual doping averaged 7.3×10¹² cm⁻² with a 32% spread, and 30 of 32 modulators still worked because device design dominated. The case is strongest for devices whose doping is not compensated by a gate, such as passive or non-volatile designs, where published tolerance data is still missing.

Existing standards cover structure (ISO/TS 21356 series), sheet resistance by THz mapping (IEC TS 62607-6-10), carrier concentration by the FET voltage-shift method for semiconducting 2D materials (IEC TS 62607-6-16), and Hall-bar mobility and sheet resistance for devices larger than 100 µm (IEC TS 62607-6-23, whose title also names carrier density). A gated transfer-length part that names doping (IEC TS 62607-6-32) is in development. As far as the public scopes show, none specifies, for pristine photonic-grade graphene, a measured gate capacitance, a Fermi level, or absorption at the operating wavelength; 6-16 and 6-32 have not yet been read in full.

Open items

Pending the human archive pass: clause 3.2 of IEC TS 62607-6-23 and the full texts of 6-16 and 6-32 (or the 6-32 committee draft) must be read to confirm whether carrier density or doping are reported quantities and whether a measured gate capacitance is required (K1); the current stage of 6-32 is unknown, and the programmes of the IEC TC 113 and ISO TC 229 groups beyond it are unmapped; a third-party search also listed IEC TS 62607-6-24:2026 and a preliminary work item on Hall resistance (6-37), neither checked; ISO/TS 21356-2 publication is not confirmed beyond the registry listings; no verified doping data was found for graphene on ferroelectric HZO, which is itself relevant to the proposal (a secondary search reported such data, but the primary paper could not be opened); Wu et al.'s 6–10×10¹² cm⁻² figure has not been re-read in the full text, although its DOI and authorship are verified; the reference "Yusuf" in references.csv is not yet identified; the authors and session of the 67 GHz modulator paper need confirming; and device-tolerance data for graphene devices whose doping is not gate-compensated is still missing (three searches found none).
